FAQ

Common questions.

Do you guarantee publication?

No. Publication decisions are made by journals and their editorial/review processes. The service focuses on publication support, preparation and submission guidance.

Can I discuss my manuscript before choosing a service?

Yes. Use the contact section to start a discussion about your manuscript, research area and current publication stage.

Do you work with review papers and conceptual papers?

Yes. Support can be tailored to the manuscript type and the requirements of the intended journal.

Will my manuscript information be kept confidential?

Manuscript information should be handled confidentially. Specific confidentiality terms can be discussed before engagement.
